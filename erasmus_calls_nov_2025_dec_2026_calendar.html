Erasmus+ — Calendar of Calls and Deadlines

Important: Dates marked as projected should be verified on the official pages and with your National Agency.

January 2026

    Confirmed deadline: 27 January 2026, 17:00 CET. Institutions of higher education apply for the ECHE to participate fully in Erasmus+ higher education activities.

February 2026

    February is typically used by applicants to finalise KA2 applications and prepare supporting documents. Check the Programme Guide for the 2026 edition.

March 2026

  • KA2 — Cooperation Partnerships (typical deadline)
    Project types: Cooperation Partnerships (Education & Training, Schools, Youth).
    Status: Most recent pattern shows an early-March deadline (first round). Applicants must verify the exact date (12:00 Brussels time).
    KA2 – Cooperation Partnerships (Programme Guide)
  • KA210 / Small-scale partnerships (schools, youth, adult education)
    Often part of the same early-March round; check national agency pages for national deadlines and variations.

April — June 2026

  • Result notifications & grant preparation
    Spring months are often used for evaluation results, clarifications, and grant preparation. Keep contact details up to date.
  • Framework Partnership Agreements / Civil Society Calls
    Some multi-year framework calls for civil society cooperation and training may have closing windows in spring — e.g. 2‑year Framework Partnership calls for 2026‑2027.
    Euro‑Access – 2‑year Framework Partnership

July — September 2026

  • Second KA2 Round (if applicable)
    Historically there may be a second KA2 deadline in autumn (e.g. 1 October). If a second round is open, prepare a fall submission.
    Monitor: Erasmus+ Funding & Calls
  • Calls for traineeships / internships periods opening
    Universities and agencies typically publish internship call windows for 2026‑2027 cohorts in summer/autumn.

October — December 2026

  • Erasmus+ Calls (ongoing monitoring)
    End-year period: new calls for the following year may be announced; program guide updates and EC communications are common in autumn.
    Follow the EU Funding & Tenders Portal for related grants and cross-programme opportunities.
  • National Agency Deadlines and Info Days
    Many National Agencies host info sessions in autumn/winter. Check your agency for webinars and support materials.
    Find your National Agency
