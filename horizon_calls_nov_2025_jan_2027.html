    Mid‑2026 often hosts calls and deadlines linked to MSCA networks, research infrastructures, and cross‑cluster topics. Always check topic pages for exact dates.

    EIC cut-off dates for 2026 are published in the EIC work programme and on the EIC website; applicants should align their timelines with published cut-offs.

September — November 2026
